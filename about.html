KLIQUESTART Metro Games

Games that work underground.

Metro rides are the perfect time for a quick game, and the worst place for internet. Signal drops between stations, speeds crawl in tunnels, and most online games freeze, buffer or never load.

Kliquestart Metro Games are built for exactly that ride. Our goal is simple: if you are on a train with weak, patchy internet, our games should still load and play.

How we build for bad internet

Tiny downloads. A whole game is smaller than a single photo, so it loads in seconds even on a slow 3G connection.

Runs on your phone, not on a server. Once a game has loaded, it keeps running if your signal drops. Lose network in a tunnel and your run carries on.

Your progress stays with you. Levels, stars and save points are stored on your device, so nothing is lost when the connection is.

Short sessions. Levels take about a minute, so you can play between stations and stop whenever your stop comes.

Our games

Our first game is Red View, a runner where you carry your data through the tunnel and save it at blue gates before you crash. More Metro Games are on the way.
