Red View

Red is your data. Blue is saved. Run through 200 years of tech and reach the save gates before you crash. 30 levels, about 30–45 minutes.

Play free

How to play

Collect the red bits as you run. Run through a blue gate and everything you carry is saved. Crash and you lose what you haven't saved, then restart from the last blue gate. You have three tries per level.

Controls

Phone

Swipe left or right to change lanes. Swipe up or tap to jump. Swipe down to slide.

Keyboard

← → change lanes · ↑ or Space jump · ↓ slide · P pause
