How to play

You are a packet of data racing down a metro tunnel. Collect the red bits as you run. They are your data, but they are not safe yet.

Each level has three blue save gates and a final save. Run through a gate and every red bit you carry turns blue. Blue data is saved for good.

Crash into an obstacle and you lose all your unsaved red data plus one of three integrity bars, then restart from the last blue gate. Save 60% of a level's data for two stars and 85% for three.

Questions

Is Red View free?

Yes. Red View is free to play in your browser with no download or sign-up.

Can I play Red View on my phone?

Yes. Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up or tap to jump, and swipe down to slide. It works on Android and iPhone browsers.

How long does Red View take to finish?

There are 30 levels of about one minute each. Most players finish in 30 to 45 minutes.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Unlocked levels and stars are saved in your browser on that device.
