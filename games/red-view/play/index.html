How to play

Keep the red, make it blue

  • Red bits are data. Grab them as you run. Until you save, they are at risk.
  • Blue gates save. Run through one and all your red data turns blue. Blue data is never lost.
  • ×Crashing costs your unsaved data and one of 3 integrity bars. You restart from the last blue gate.
  • ★Save 60% of a level's data for 2 stars, 85% for 3. Each era brings a new obstacle.
